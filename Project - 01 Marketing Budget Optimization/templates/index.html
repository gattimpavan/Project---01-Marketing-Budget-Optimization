Phase 1: Week 1 - 2

Immediate Bleed Defunding

  • Defund Programmatic Display: Slashing monthly budget from $85k to $25k immediately halts a negative ROI drain.
  • Cap TikTok Prospecting: Restrict TikTok to strict lookalike audiences; eliminate broad demographic bleed.
  • Purge Affiliate Low-Quality Placements: Review publisher conversion logs to remove coupon scraper arbitrage.
  • Expected Savings: $80,000 / month in wasted capital reclaimed.
Phase 2: Week 3 - 6

Scale High-Elasticity Channels

  • Scale Google Search (+ $45,000/mo): High intent capture with strong payback (< 2.2 months) and high LTV ($1,450).
  • Scale LinkedIn B2B (+ $25,000/mo): Premium enterprise customer segment yielding $2,400+ LTV and 4.2x LTV:CAC.
  • Expand Automated Email Retargeting (+ $10,000/mo): Highest ROAS engine (3.13x) with minimal CAC.
  • Projected Revenue Uplift: + $93,800 / month (+ $1.12M annualized).
Phase 3: Ongoing

Autonomous AI Governance

  • Automated Weekly SLSQP Solvers: System recalibrates channel allocations weekly as conversion data flows in.
  • Dynamic Marginal ROAS Guardrails: Trigger auto-throttling whenever a channel's marginal ROAS falls below 1.15x.
  • LTV:CAC Safety Circuit Breakers: Any campaign with LTV:CAC < 1.8x over 30 days is automatically paused for review.
  • Executive PDF Generation: Automated monthly PDF briefing delivery to C-suite leadership.
